# Atlas metálico de XOR/XNOR

Arquivo fonte: `Logic_Parity_Metal_Texture.png`, 512×512, totalmente opaco.

Criado em 22/10/2026 com a habilidade **imagegen** e a ferramenta integrada **image_gen**, em uma geração e um refinamento. A arte gerada foi normalizada tecnicamente para 512×512 por System.Drawing; não houve geração por CLI/API alternativa. Os PNGs anteriores de outros componentes não foram usados como alvos de edição.

A ordem é uma grade 4×4: prata e sombras na primeira linha; aço e destaques de símbolo na segunda; indicador apagado/aceso na terceira; fios e grafite na quarta. O runtime usa patches centrais definidos em `tools/parity_material.py`. O PNG é fonte de arte; os geradores não recriam seus pixels.

## Prompt de geração

Create one production game texture atlas PNG, square 1024 x 1024 pixels. It is a precise 4 columns by 4 rows material swatch grid, sixteen equal square cells, grid fills the whole image edge to edge. No text, letters, labels, grid lines, gutters, borders, logos, objects, perspective or transparent pixels. Every cell is a front-facing flat material texture, not a 3D sphere or cube. Crisp restrained pixel-art / Hytale blocky game material style. Each cell has subtle fine brushed grain and a soft baked highlight-to-shadow variation, but its CENTRAL HALF must remain within its specified material color family. This atlas will be UV-mapped to small metal logic gates and needs highly legible contrast. Row 1 left to right: (1) BRIGHT cool polished SILVER metal, pale neutral gray around #d4dae0, (2) medium-light silver steel #acb5c0, (3) medium steel shadow #7c8997, (4) dark metal shadow #4c5968. Row 2 left to right: (1) medium cool brushed steel #8b9aab for a contrasting XOR symbol curve, (2) bright metallic edge highlight #c4ced8, (3) dark machined steel #576575, (4) neutral silver #a4afb9. Row 3 left to right: (1) UNLIT glass indicator, muted DARK SLATE GRAY with subtle blue cast #465565, decisively much darker than the silver body and not emissive, (2) darker unlit indicator side #2f3c4c, (3) LIT indicator vivid saturated bright RED #f32118, center must have R above 215 and G/B below 60, no white or yellow highlights, (4) deep saturated red indicator side #ba100c, center must remain strongly red, never gray or pink. Row 4 left to right: (1) dark charcoal cable jacket #242a31, (2) almost-black cable side #10151b, (3) muted graphite #343e49, (4) steel edge shadow #697888. Texture variations should be detailed enough to read as metal yet restrained enough for small game components. All sixteen cells fully opaque, no cast shadows between cells. The dark unlit glass and the bright silver base MUST look visibly different.

## Prompt de refinamento

Technical production correction of this exact game texture atlas. Preserve its sixteen material swatches, their 4-by-4 layout and their colors/contrast. Output a PNG canvas EXACTLY 1024 pixels wide and EXACTLY 1024 pixels high, with the swatch cell boundaries EXACTLY x=256,512,768 and y=256,512,768. This pixel dimension is a hard engine requirement: do not produce a 1254 by 1254 image. Fully opaque. No added borders or labels. Keep the light silver metal in the top left, dark unlit gray-blue lens in row3col1, vivid red lens in row3col3, and dark cables in bottom row. Reduce the overly large grain into restrained fine brushed-metal texture suitable for small pixel-art Hytale game models, preserving baked highlights and shadows. This is a normalization and polish of the supplied atlas, not a new arrangement.

Os prompts registram a solicitação à ferramenta. A saída bruta foi 1254×1254; o PNG de entrega foi normalizado para 512×512. A escolha de resolução de entrega é uma decisão de integração, não uma afirmação de que Hytale exige exatamente 1024×1024.
